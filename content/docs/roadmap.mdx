## What works today

| You can | State |
|---|---|
| Install qq and build, test and run xo-space and innernet | works, on Linux x86_64 toolchains |
| Give a repo its own commands | works |
| Onboard a new repo with `qqsync init` | works |
| Land a product change through the gate and merge queue | works; one approval needed |
| See whether `main` is green | works for xo-space and innernet |
| Get lkgr and the canary for xo-space and innernet | works, with a daily backstop for skipped runs |
| Resolve what the canary names with `qqinstall` | works |
| Automatic reverts | not yet: waits on the gardener App |
| Installs that follow a channel | not yet: every install follows `main` |

## v0: the exit test

All five must hold together:

1. All 13 repos exist and are public, each with CODEOWNERS, a generated
   presubmit and a merge queue.
   **Today:** partly; the infra repos' presubmits are hand-written.
2. xo-space and innernet are gated from their `infra/repo.toml`, and a
   deliberately red pull request is refused in each.
   **Today:** partly; CI runs stand-in commands, not the recipes, and no
   refusal is recorded yet.
3. A daily canary runs 7 days in a row with no human touch; a planted bad
   canary is held; a rollback drill takes under 10 minutes.
   **Today:** not yet.
4. A planted breaking commit is reverted automatically.
   **Today:** not started; it needs the gardener App.
5. The scorecard is computed by a script, not typed.
   **Today:** built.

## After v0

| Version | Delivers |
|---|---|
| **v1** | an unattended canary with soak time, automatic rollback and a circuit breaker (bar: 14 green canaries in a row with no human touch); the dev channel, promoted weekly with human approval; fuzzing; PostHog as a health signal, never the gate; flake handling; hermetic builds aiming at 90% cache hits |
| **v2** | Launchpad and remote execution (bar: gate p50 under 10 minutes and p90 under 20 for 4 weeks); perf bisection of a planted 10% regression; agents on every rotation; stable every two weeks, each promotion approved by suraj; installs that follow a channel |

## Decided, and still open

suraj settled the compute ceiling, the canary hour (06:17 UTC) and the revert
cap on 2026-10-06; they are not written into config yet. Still open: who uses
the canary beyond quirq's test environments, PostHog's plan and scope, and
whether an agent may roll stable back alone on a health breach.
