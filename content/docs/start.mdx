## Names you will see

- **XO Space**: the app your agents work in. It runs on your computer.
- **XO Cloud**: the same app, run for you in the cloud.
- **quirq**: our company, and also the [meter that measures the work agents deliver](/docs/quirq).
- **qq**: the tool we use to build, test and ship our own code.
