---
title: Share
description: Invite another XO user to a copy of a space or a project. They see the invite, accept it into their own spaces, or reject it.
icon: Share2
---

`xo share` puts an invite in another XO user's list. Nothing else of theirs
changes, and nothing of theirs is shown to you.

```sh
xo share space:dev bob                # invite bob to a copy of dev: its name and projects
xo share project:juanfont/headscale bob
```

Only GitHub projects are shared, since nobody else could install a local one,
and a space's local projects are left out of its invite.

## Accept or reject

```sh
xo list shared                                # the invites waiting on you, each with an id
xo accept 0123abcd0123abcd                    # a shared space: shows it, asks, then adds it as yours
xo accept 0123abcd0123abcd --as dev2          # the same, when you already have a space called dev
xo accept 4567cdef4567cdef --space dev        # a shared project: into your space dev, at main
xo reject 0123abcd0123abcd                    # take it off; its sender can share it again
```

- Accepting a space copies its name and projects into your own spaces, as a
  new space. Accepting a project puts it in the space you name with
  `--space`, at `--ref` (default `main`). Either way its projects join your
  projects too, so `xo install` can use them.
- After that, changes on either side do not reach the other.
- An invite sent again gets a new id, so `xo accept` never takes something
  other than what you saw. `xo list shared` shows each sender's username.
- A share to a username that does not exist, or whose list is full, gets the
  same "could not share" answer. Invites together take at most 1.5 KB of the
  6 KB your account holds; accepting or rejecting one gives its room back.
