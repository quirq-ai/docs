`xo` is the command line for [XO Cloud](https://dev.xo.builders). Every XO
Cloud call is a command, and xo sends your XO API key on each request without
ever printing it. xo is separate from qq: you can use either without the
other.

With xo you:

- **create spaces** on XO Cloud, such as a Claude Code, Codex or OpenClaw
  space, or a space that lives only on your machine;
- **keep projects in a space**: your GitHub repos, each at a branch, tag or
  commit, cloned and pushed with your own git;
- **share a space or a project** with another XO user, who accepts it into
  their own spaces.

## One login, three services

One `xo login` logs you in to all three. `xo status` shows each one and
whether you are logged in.

| Service | Address | What it does | You log in with |
|---|---|---|---|
| `me` | `https://xo.quirq.dev` | the xo server: answers `xo me`, records your projects and spaces in your XO account, passes creates on to XO Cloud | your XO API key, which `xo login` gets when you sign in to XO in your browser |
| `xo` | `https://dev.xo.builders` | XO Cloud: creates the spaces | the same key, through the xo server |
| `github` | `https://github.com` | your GitHub account, through the GitHub CLI (`gh`) | gh's own login, in your browser |

xo keeps your keys only on your machine, and the xo server stores none. Your
XO key is still sent to the xo server and to XO Cloud on every call, and a
create copies it into the new space.
