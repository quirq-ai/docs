## The three versions

| Version | Delivers | Items |
|---|---|---|
| **v0** | all 13 repos in a base version; products gated; daily canary; auto-revert | 51 |
| **v1** | unattended canary, dev channel, fuzzing, PostHog, flakes, hermetic builds | about 45 |
| **v2** | Launchpad, remote execution, perf bisection, stable, installer | 25 |

The v1 count is approximate: infra-config's plan lists 44 items, and a
later count has 45. Only v0's items are published.

## v0: the exit test

All five must hold together:

1. All 13 repos exist and are public, each with CODEOWNERS, a generated
   presubmit and a merge queue.
2. xo-space and innernet are gated from their `infra/repo.toml`, and a
   deliberately red pull request is refused in each.
3. A daily canary runs 7 days in a row with no human touch; a planted bad
   canary is held; a rollback drill takes under 10 minutes.
4. A planted breaking commit is reverted automatically.
5. The scorecard is computed by a script, not typed.

## What is built

| Part | State |
|---|---|
| The 13 repos, infra-config and its `validate` check | built |
| Builders delivered to xo-space, innernet and website | built; org-wide drift check off (GitHub Free) |

Per exit-test line, see [Where qq stands](/docs/qq#where-qq-stands).

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="v1 and v2 in more detail">
    **v1:** the canary gains soak time, automatic rollback and a circuit
    breaker (the bar: 14 green canaries in a row with no human touch). Dev is
    promoted weekly with human approval. PostHog is a health signal, never
    the gate. Hermetic builds aim at 90% cache hits.

    **v2** moves execution to Launchpad, adds remote execution (the bar:
    gate p50 under 10 minutes and p90 under 20 for 4 weeks) and perf
    bisection of a planted 10% regression, and puts agents on every
    rotation. Stable is a two-week target with each promotion approved by
    suraj.

    The likely critical path through v0: manifests, adapters, builders,
    gate, onboarding, lkgr, channels, then the daily canary.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Still open

suraj settled the compute ceiling, the canary hour (06:17 UTC) and the
revert cap on 2026-10-06; they are not in config yet. Still open: who uses the canary
beyond quirq's test environments, PostHog's plan and scope, and whether an
agent may roll stable back alone on a health breach.
