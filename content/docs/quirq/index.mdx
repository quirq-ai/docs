<Lead>Anyone can launch an agent on XO. Very few can say whether it's working. quirq is a proposed way to count the work agents deliver. Each unit is checked against what actually changed, not reported by the agent.</Lead>

The token meters the machine side completely: cost, energy, carbon. Nothing
meters the work side — outcomes delivered at human-assigned value. That single
gap is why AI budgets climb while ROI stays undemonstrable. quirq is a proposed unit
to close it.

<TwoMeters />

## The unit in one paragraph

A human owner defines an outcome with a machine-checkable **definition of done**
and budgets it at value **B**: the price they would pay for the outcome to
exist. The task carries B *potential* quirqs. An environment snapshots the world
before execution (S₀) and after (S₁), scores completion **V ∈ [0, 1]** against
the definition of done, and **mints V · B quirqs** of delivered work, recorded
in a tamper-evident ledger with everything production consumed: inference,
compute, API calls, storage, and human intervention.
