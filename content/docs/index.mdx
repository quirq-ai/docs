## Quick Answers

<Accordions>
	<Accordion title="Is XO Space free?">
		Yes. **XO Space** is open source, and you can run it on your computer for free with one command.

		**XO Cloud** on [app.xo.builders](https://app.xo.builders) runs it for you on a cloud machine; you pick a plan when you sign up. Either way, you bring your own model key.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Do I need my own model key?">
		Yes. Use an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter, or sign in with
		your Claude or ChatGPT subscription. XO does not sell model credits.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="What is the difference between XO Space and XO Cloud?">
		**XO Space** is the open-source app your agents work in. **XO Cloud** runs XO Space for you on a cloud machine, so you don't install anything. See the [overview](/docs/cloud/overview).
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="How do I see what my agents are doing?">
		Install Space on your computer with `curl -fsSL quirq.ai/install | sh` (or add it as an agent skill), then open `localhost:5002` to see your agents' sessions and tool use.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Where is my data kept?">
		With Space on your computer, your work stays on your computer. If you turn on [usage reporting](/docs/space/space-walk/setup#usage-reporting), Space sends daily usage summaries. On **XO Cloud**, your work is on your cloud machine.
	</Accordion>
</Accordions>
